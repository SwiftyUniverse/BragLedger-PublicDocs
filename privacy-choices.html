BragLedger

Privacy Choices

BragLedger keeps your gameplay data on this device. You can export it or wipe it from Settings.

Last updated: May 1, 2026

These choices reflect the current local-only build. Re-review them before shipping any new always-on off-device data path beyond user-initiated sharing/export.

1. Manage data in the app

  • Use Export Data in Settings if you want a portable JSON copy.
  • Use Delete All BragLedger Data in Settings to remove app data from this device.
  • Remove saved players from the Saved Players screen.
  • Remove saved teams from the Saved Teams screen.
  • Delete saved game types if you no longer want those setups available.
  • Delete custom leaderboards if you no longer want their manual tallies.
  • Delete the app from your device if you want to remove the local copy from that device.

2. Understand sync and retention

  • BragLedger keeps gameplay data on this device during normal app use.
  • Saved roster items, match history, game setups, and leaderboard data remain until you remove them in the app or use Delete All BragLedger Data.

3. Control sharing

  • BragLedger creates a share image or export archive only when you explicitly use the iOS share sheet or export flow.
  • You choose the destination. If you do not share or export, the app does not send that snapshot or archive to a developer-run server in the current version.

4. App Store billing

  • App downloads, billing, and refunds are handled through Apple.
  • To manage App Store billing, refunds, or purchase-record questions, use Apple's purchase support and your Apple account tools.

5. Ask a question or make a request

If you have a privacy question or want to make a privacy-related request, contact:

support@zenjutsu.net

Many access, correction, export, and deletion actions can be handled directly in the app because BragLedger exposes Export Data and Delete All BragLedger Data in Settings.

6. Read the full policy

For the complete notice, go to the BragLedger Privacy Policy.

The same privacy policy is also available in the app from Settings.
