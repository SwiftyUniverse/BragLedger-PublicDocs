# BragLedger Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Canonical policy: https://swiftyuniverse.github.io/BragLedger-PublicDocs/privacy-policy.html

BragLedger keeps an offline local copy. While Plus sync is active, your entire record book, including Personal and Club records, mirrors through your private iCloud database after disclosure and verified access.

## Quick Summary

- Free use without enabled Plus sync stays local.
- Enabled Plus sync covers Personal and Club records in the same private record book.
- Sharing, export, and support email are user-directed.
- No ads, tracking, analytics SDK, or BragLedger account system.
- GitHub Pages logs website visitor IP addresses for security.
- Version 2.0 and older 1.x behavior are distinguished below.

## 1. Who we are

BragLedger is published by Conner Glasgow, who is responsible for the personal data handled by the publisher under this policy. In this policy, "BragLedger," "we," "us," and "our" refer to that publisher.

Privacy contact: support@zenjutsu.net

## 2. Scope and territory coverage

This policy describes personal data handling in BragLedger 2.0, including the free scorekeeper, BragLedger Plus private iCloud sync, user-directed sharing or export, support email, and the public privacy and support pages.

BragLedger 1.x keeps gameplay records on the device without automatic private iCloud sync, apart from retired sync builds whose cloud copy can be removed using Legacy iCloud Cleanup. The Plus sync description applies to version 2.0.

BragLedger is not offered for download in China, Brazil, South Korea, or Vietnam. These distribution choices do not remove rights you have under applicable law.

## 3. Data we handle

BragLedger uses the data you enter so the app can remember your games, rosters, Clubs, competitions, statistics, and leaderboards. An organizer may enter information about other players; that information comes from the organizer, not from those players directly.

You can use nicknames instead of real names. Avoid entering sensitive information or details that are not needed for scorekeeping. You can use the free scorekeeper without enabling Plus sync.

- Gameplay identity data such as player, team, Club, Season, and Game Night names; colors; memberships; default rosters; and actual team-lineup snapshots.
- Match and leaderboard data such as scores, winners and ties, timestamps, rounds, game types, opponent and teammate facts, and custom-board events.
- Share content such as receipt images, leaderboard snapshots, captions, and winner labels that are rendered locally.
- StoreKit product and verified entitlement status, original app version used for legacy access, sync status, tutorial flags, and migration state. Entitlement evidence is not exported.
- If you email support, your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send are handled to respond to your request. Avoid sending names, archives, or receipts unless needed for support.
- The public pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. These pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts.

## 4. How we use data

We use app data to provide the scorekeeper and the organizer features you choose. We do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

- Save the players, teams, Clubs, game setups, matches, and manual boards you create.
- Derive receipts, statistics, standings, rivalries, trophy history, and recaps from match facts.
- Synchronize your entire record book, including Personal and Club records, through the private iCloud database associated with your Apple/iCloud account while Plus sync is active.
- Create a shareable snapshot or JSON archive when you explicitly ask for one.
- Remember your local app settings and onboarding state.
- Respond to support and privacy requests you send us.
- Make the public privacy and support pages available through GitHub Pages and support their security.

## 5. When data leaves your device

Without enabled Plus sync, gameplay records are not automatically uploaded by BragLedger. While Plus sync is active, the entire record book, including Personal games, Club records, rosters, game types, match history, and manual leaderboards, is automatically sent to and retrieved from Apple's private CloudKit service after purchase disclosure or the required 2.0 migration disclosure.

Separately, data can leave your device when you choose to share a receipt, recap, leaderboard snapshot, or JSON archive.

Emailing support sends the content you choose to provide through your email service and Google Workspace, which hosts the support mailbox. Conner Glasgow can access that correspondence to respond. BragLedger has no in-app support form or automatic support upload.

When you send something to another app, cloud drive, message thread, or service, that destination handles the copy you sent under its own privacy terms.

Apple's privacy policy, GitHub's privacy statement and Google Workspace's privacy information describe their data handling and international safeguards. These providers may process data outside your country. Contact support with questions about the handling or transfer of support correspondence.

- Apple handles App Store downloads, subscriptions, billing, refunds, verified transactions, and private iCloud storage under Apple's policies and service terms.
- BragLedger does not run a separate developer-operated analytics, advertising, account, or gameplay backend.
- User-entered names use CloudKit-encrypted fields where Core Data/CloudKit supports them. BragLedger is not end-to-end or zero-knowledge encrypted.

## 6. Legal bases where local law requires them

Where a privacy law requires a legal basis for handling personal data, we rely on the basis that matches the specific activity.

- Performance of a contract: to provide the app user with requested scoring, subscriptions, restore and management, private synchronization, and organizer features.
- Your direction or consent where applicable: when you continue through the migration disclosure, share a snapshot, export a file, or otherwise send data to a destination.
- Legitimate interests where applicable: to support an organizer's ordinary group game records, respond to requests, maintain app and website security, preserve local settings, prevent destructive migrations, and support reliable synchronization. Minimize names and other identifying details when keeping group records.
- Legal obligations: if we must keep or disclose limited information to comply with law.

## 7. Retention and deletion

BragLedger keeps local and private-iCloud data until you remove it through the applicable in-app control. Ending Plus stops mirroring at a safe reload but does not delete preserved records.

Some delete actions in the app hide or archive saved items while preserving older match history so past game results remain readable.

Turning sync off stops future mirroring while keeping local records and the existing cloud copy. Cloud-only deletion verifies a recovery archive and keeps local records. Delete Everywhere deletes the app-managed local record book and the current private-cloud zone, but not Apple's purchase history, a retired sync build's cloud copy, device backups managed by Apple, or copies you shared or exported. Use Legacy iCloud Cleanup separately for a retired cloud copy.

A verified recovery archive is kept on this device before migration or cloud-only deletion. Treat that archive as a copy of your record book. Deleting all local app data also removes app-managed recovery archives on this device.

Record-book deletion can leave minimal local access evidence, including a verified legacy-purchase flag and verification date, so earlier purchasers keep lifetime Plus. This evidence is not included in record-book exports or private-cloud gameplay records.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by contacting support.

GitHub controls retention of its website security logs under its privacy statement. In-app record deletion does not delete support correspondence or provider security logs, and it does not cancel an Apple subscription.

If you export or share data, copies held by the destination you chose are governed by that destination and may remain there until you delete them with that service or app.

## 8. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, object to, or complain about the handling of your personal data.

Many of those rights can be exercised directly in the app. The publisher does not receive your local or private-iCloud record book through the app and cannot search or edit it remotely. If another organizer entered your information, ask that organizer to correct or remove it; support can explain the available controls.

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing. Turn sync off to stop future mirroring; use cloud deletion separately to remove its existing copy.

You may complain to your local privacy regulator, including the ICO in the UK or a national data protection authority in the EEA. You can contact support before making a complaint, but you do not have to.

- Use Export Data in Settings to create a portable JSON copy.
- Turn private sync off while keeping local data, delete only the cloud copy, or use Delete Everywhere for app-managed local and new private-cloud records.
- Edit, correct, retire, merge, or anonymize supported roster identities and records in the app.
- Stop optional sharing at any time by not using the share or export controls.
- Contact support@zenjutsu.net if you need help with a privacy request.

## 9. Children

BragLedger is a general-audience app and is not directed to children.

An organizer may enter another person's name, including a minor's name. Only enter information you are authorized to use, obtain any permission required by applicable law, prefer nicknames, and use correction, anonymization, or deletion controls when information should not remain. Contact support if you believe a child's information has been improperly provided to the publisher.
