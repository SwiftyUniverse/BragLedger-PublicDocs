BRAGLEDGER

Support

Need help with a game, your saved records, Plus access, or privacy? Email support@zenjutsu.net.

Please include your app version and a short description of what happened. Do not email player names, full game archives, or purchase receipts unless support specifically asks for them through an appropriate channel.

Scores and saved records

For a portable copy, open BragLedger Settings and use Export Data. Settings also contains controls to correct records or remove app data. Check the action's explanation before deleting a record.

Plus and purchases

Use Restore Purchases in the app if an eligible purchase is missing. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account's subscription settings. For an App Store charge or refund, use Apple's purchase support.

Private iCloud sync

In version 2.0, enabled Plus sync includes your entire record book, including Personal and Club records. Check the sync status in Settings before changing or deleting anything. Keep an export of important records. Contact support if the same organizer's devices show different results or recovery is unclear.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy and Privacy Choices. Email support@zenjutsu.net with a privacy question or request.
