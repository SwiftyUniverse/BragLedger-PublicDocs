1. Who we are

BragLedger is published by Conner Glasgow. In this policy, "BragLedger," "we," "us," and "our" refer to the publisher of the app.

Privacy contact: support@zenjutsu.net

2. Scope and territory coverage

This policy describes how BragLedger 2.0 handles personal data in the free scorekeeper, BragLedger Plus private iCloud sync, and user-directed sharing or export.

BragLedger 1.x keeps gameplay records on the device without automatic private iCloud sync, apart from retired sync builds whose cloud copy can be removed using Legacy iCloud Cleanup. The Plus sync description applies to version 2.0.

This policy does not cover use in China, Brazil, South Korea, or Vietnam.

3. Data we handle

BragLedger uses the data you enter so the app can remember your games, rosters, Clubs, competitions, statistics, and leaderboards.

  • Gameplay identity data such as player, team, Club, Season, and Game Night names; colors; memberships; default rosters; and actual team-lineup snapshots.
  • Match and leaderboard data such as scores, winners and ties, timestamps, rounds, game types, opponent and teammate facts, and custom-board events.
  • Share content such as receipt images, leaderboard snapshots, captions, and winner labels that are rendered locally.
  • StoreKit product and verified entitlement status, original app version used for legacy access, sync status, tutorial flags, and migration state. Entitlement evidence is not exported.
  • If you email support, your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send are handled to respond to your request. Avoid sending names, archives, or receipts unless needed for support.

4. How we use data

We use app data to provide the scorekeeper and the organizer features you choose.

  • Save the players, teams, Clubs, game setups, matches, and manual boards you create.
  • Derive receipts, statistics, standings, rivalries, trophy history, and recaps from match facts.
  • Synchronize your entire record book, including Personal and Club records, through the private iCloud database associated with your Apple/iCloud account while Plus sync is active.
  • Create a shareable snapshot or JSON archive when you explicitly ask for one.
  • Remember your local app settings and onboarding state.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests you send us.

5. When data leaves your device

Without enabled Plus sync, gameplay records are not automatically uploaded by BragLedger. While Plus sync is active, the entire record book, including Personal games, Club records, rosters, game types, match history, and manual leaderboards, is automatically sent to and retrieved from Apple's private CloudKit service after purchase disclosure or the required 2.0 migration disclosure.

Separately, data can leave your device when you choose to share a receipt, recap, leaderboard snapshot, or JSON archive.

Emailing support sends the content you choose to provide through your email service and the support mailbox provider. BragLedger has no in-app support form or automatic support upload.

When you send something to another app, cloud drive, message thread, or service, that destination handles the copy you sent under its own privacy terms.

  • Apple handles App Store downloads, subscriptions, billing, refunds, verified transactions, and private iCloud storage under Apple's policies and service terms.
  • BragLedger does not run a separate developer-operated analytics, advertising, account, or gameplay backend.
  • User-entered names use CloudKit-encrypted fields where Core Data/CloudKit supports them. BragLedger is not end-to-end or zero-knowledge encrypted.

6. Legal bases where local law requires them

Where a privacy law requires a legal basis for handling personal data, we rely on the basis that matches the specific activity.

  • Performance of a contract: to provide scoring, subscriptions, restore and management, private synchronization, and organizer features.
  • Your direction or consent where applicable: when you continue through the migration disclosure, share a snapshot, export a file, or otherwise send data to a destination.
  • Legitimate interests where applicable: to maintain app integrity, preserve local settings, prevent destructive migrations, and support reliable synchronization.
  • Legal obligations: if we must keep or disclose limited information to comply with law.

7. Retention and deletion

BragLedger keeps local and private-iCloud data until you remove it through the applicable in-app control. Ending Plus stops mirroring at a safe reload but does not delete preserved records.

Some delete actions in the app hide or archive saved items while preserving older match history so past game results remain readable.

Turning sync off keeps the local store. Cloud-only deletion verifies a recovery archive and keeps local records. Delete Everywhere removes app-managed local data and the current private-cloud zone, but not Apple's purchase history, a retired sync build's cloud copy, device backups managed by Apple, or copies you shared or exported. Use Legacy iCloud Cleanup separately for a retired cloud copy.

A verified recovery archive is kept on this device before migration or cloud-only deletion. Treat that archive as a copy of your record book. Deleting all local app data also removes app-managed recovery archives on this device.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by contacting support.

If you export or share data, copies held by the destination you chose are governed by that destination and may remain there until you delete them with that service or app.

8. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, object to, or complain about the handling of your personal data.

Many of those rights can be exercised directly in the app.

  • Use Export Data in Settings to create a portable JSON copy.
  • Turn private sync off while keeping local data, delete only the cloud copy, or use Delete Everywhere for app-managed local and new private-cloud records.
  • Edit, correct, retire, merge, or anonymize supported roster identities and records in the app.
  • Stop optional sharing at any time by not using the share or export controls.
  • Contact support@zenjutsu.net if you need help with a privacy request.

9. Children

BragLedger is a general-audience app and is not directed to children.

An organizer may enter another person's name, including a minor's name. Only enter information you are authorized to use, avoid unnecessary identifying details, and use correction, anonymization, or deletion controls when information should not remain.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features, data flows, launch territories, or legal requirements change.

When we do, we will update the policy text and the last-updated date before the changed version ships.
